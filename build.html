under the hood

How we use Claude

Privacy first. Names, national ID numbers, addresses, phone numbers and bank details are masked on our side before any text reaches the model.

Strict, plain-language explanations. Claude explains each official letter in simple Turkish under fixed rules: say only what the letter says, never invent deadlines, amounts or penalties, flag uncertainty, and label urgency green / yellow / red. Letters mentioning enforcement, lawsuits or fines are always referred to an accountant or lawyer.

Reviews the owner controls. With the owner's permission, Claude summarises their own Google reviews each month and drafts replies that are posted only after the owner approves them.

Our first prototype is shown in the live demo: a sample municipal letter is masked locally and explained by Claude; the answer is shown unedited.
